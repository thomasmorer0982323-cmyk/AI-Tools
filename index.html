AI Tools for Language Teaching

Welcome to your guide for choosing the best AI-tool for your needs. Discover a range of AI-tools designed to support both teachers and students when it comes to languages. From creating pictures and assisting with writing to making a lesson plan. This website will assist you to find resources that enhance the efficiency of language learning.
Advantages of AI in education: 1. Enhanced learning outcomes. AI has the potential to transform language education by improving overall learning results. However, nuance is important: AI is most effective when used as a complementary tool alongside traditional teaching methods and human guidance, not on its own. 2. Constant accessibility. AI tools are available 24/7, acting as an assistant to both teachers and students. It can be accessed both inside and outside the classroom. 3.Workload reduction. Especially for teachers, AI tools can support them by taking over some time-consuming tasks, such as grading assignments or adapting texts to specific students’ needs.
