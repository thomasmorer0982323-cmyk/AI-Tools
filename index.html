AI Tools for Language Teaching

Welcome to your guide for choosing the best AI tool for your needs. This website introduces a range of AI tools designed to support both teachers and students in language learning. You will find resources for tasks such as creating images, assisting with writing, and developing lesson plans all aimed at making language learning more efficient.

Instead of focusing on general AI chatbots such as ChatGPT, which most people already know, this guide highlights more specific tools designed for particular purposes. All tools included offer a free version, as the focus is on accessibility for both students and teachers.

I tested every AI tool on this website as part of my lesson preparation during my teaching internship. For example, I wrote an English text for my class and checked it using writing assistants. I also created presentations on common prepositions and designed exercises on future tenses. For the image and video generation tools, I took inspiration from my vocabulary lessons on travelling.

Advantages of AI in language education:

1. Enhanced learning outcomes AI has the potential to transform language education by improving overall learning results. However, nuance is important: AI is most effective when used as a complementary tool alongside traditional teaching methods and human guidance, not on its own.

2. Constant accessibility AI tools are available 24/7, acting as an assistant to both teachers and students. It can be accessed both inside and outside the classroom.

3. Workload reduction Especially for teachers, AI tools can support them by taking over some time-consuming tasks, such as grading assignments or adapting texts to specific students’ needs.

Disadvantages of AI in language education:

1. Overreliance on AI There are some consequences to consider when it comes to AI in education. A major issue is that students may become too dependent on AI tools, which can prevent them from engaging in the cognitive processes necessary to develop skills such as grammar. In addition, relying heavily on AI may reduce original thinking and creativity, eventually turning students into passive learners.

2. Inaccurate Information AI tools are known to ‘hallucinate’ from time to time, meaning they can show information that is not true. It’s important that students and teachers learn to critically evaluate AI-generated content rather than trusting it unconditionally.

3. Social Challenges As of today, there is not enough research on the long-term effects of AI tools in education, but there are worries that AI may reduce valuable interaction between teachers and students. Some believe that excessive use of AI could eventually affect students’ social and emotional development.
